1. Definition & Functional Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are compact alphanumeric text identifiers stored on your computing device or mobile terminal by your internet browser when interacting with digital portals. At NoodleTable, we employ functional and analytical cookies to facilitate seamless navigation, preserve active reservation session tokens, and recall guest interface preferences. These digital mechanisms enable our server architecture to distinguish your session from other diners without retaining unnecessary personally identifiable dossiers.

Functional cookies deployed across our website are strictly necessary to deliver core digital services, such as retaining your selected dinner party size, booking timestamps, and accessibility visual configurations as you navigate between pages. Without these foundational cookies enabled, our reservation booking engine and interactive noodle hydration calculator cannot function correctly. These operational tokens expire automatically upon conclusion of your browsing session.
